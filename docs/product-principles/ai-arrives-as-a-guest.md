# AI arrives as a guest

AI is here to extend a person's reach, not to claim their workspace as its own.
It should speak plainly about what it understands, what it is proposing, and
where its confidence ends. A helpful system leaves room for refusal, revision,
and a different idea.

This principle applies as much to tone as to behavior. The product should feel
like a careful collaborator, not an authority performing certainty.
