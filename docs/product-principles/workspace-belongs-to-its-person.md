# The workspace belongs to its person

AI-Mo-To begins with a simple position: a person's work should remain legible,
portable, and close at hand. The product may help organize a workspace, but it
does not turn that workspace into a black box or a service that must be trusted
from afar.

When the product changes, it should leave the person better able to understand
what they have, where it lives, and how to continue without ceremony.
